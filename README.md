## What is TillyTasky?

If you love the feeling of checking things off a to-do list, TillyTasky turns getting stuff done into a game where you try to beat your high score from the day before.

A.k.a. productive dopamine hits. Who doesn’t love that?

Best experienced on desktop. Use TillyTasky for work, school, personal life, and everything in between.

## Why TillyTasky?

> “I use habit and routine to make my life possible.” - Leonard Shelby, *Memento* (2000)

I’ve been a Notion user for over six years, starting back in my junior (COVID) year of high school. For a while, it worked great for managing my tasks.

However, every time I reset my planner for a brand new school year, I’d slowly stop checking it until I was back to just relying on my school calendar. It was always the same static words in the same boring table.

I realized a checkmark on a list wasn’t enough.

I needed a game. Something to be excited about. Something that would keep me going without burning me out.

While hunting for my next tech job in the AI rat race, I started keeping a daily tally of submitted applications, challenging myself to beat yesterday's count. Because let's be real: recruiting is a numbers game.

So why not bring this “game” to everyday tasks?

I scoured template after template, but most were obsessed with tracking the same five basic habits forever. I wanted a space where I could throw in *"booked therapy!"*, hit complete, and get confetti blasted across my screen.

And just like that, **TillyTasky** was born.
